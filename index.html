Everything on one screen. Nothing to configure.

Gram trades Gantt charts and spreadsheets for a grid you can read in three seconds.

Drag it where it fits

Drop a task on any month. Gram finds a free row and draws the span instantly. Move it until the picture makes sense.

Size work in dev-months

A task of size 3 spans three months of one person. No hours, no story points, no ceremony.

Capacity per month

Set how many people each month has. Rows over capacity turn red before you commit to anything.

Share with a link

One click gives you a link. Anyone who has it sees the same plan and edits it live. No accounts, no invites.

Local first

Your plan lives in your browser until you share it. Loads instantly, works offline, and never asks you to log in.

Made to be looked at

Three palettes, side or bottom layout, a today marker and done states. A plan you'll actually want to open.

Three steps. Maybe two minutes.

Gram is built for the planning conversation, not for the report after it.

Add projects and tasks

Create a project, add its tasks, and set each one's size in dev-months.

Drag them onto the timeline

Place tasks on the 12-month grid. Adjust monthly capacity and watch what overflows.

Share the link

Hit Share and send the link. Everyone sees the same plan and can move things around.

One link. No accounts.

When you're ready, Share publishes your plan to a private link. Whoever has it can view and edit, and changes sync in seconds. Keep the link private and it stays private.
